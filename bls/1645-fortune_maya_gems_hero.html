What we do not collect

The Application does not collect location information (precise or approximate). It does not request location permission and does not use GPS, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cell positioning. IP address, if processed by a network or analytics service, is not used to determine or store your location.

The Service Provider does not collect payment card numbers. The Service Provider does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with data brokers.

The Application stores progress, scores, settings, and local logs on the device (for example in on-device preferences). If you do not sign in, this data is not uploaded as an account profile. Uninstalling the Application deletes this local copy.

iOS (App Store)

This section applies only if you downloaded the Application from the Apple App Store. It does not describe the Google Play binary.

App Tracking Transparency (Guideline 5.1.2)

On iOS, Adjust may request permission to track for attribution and install measurement only. That request, if shown, is Apple’s ATT prompt. The Application does not use tracking permission for personalized advertising, remarketing, or sharing with data brokers.

If you allow tracking, IDFA may be used together with IDFV for attribution. Under Apple’s definition this is tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", IDFA is not used. The Application still works in full; features are not gated on ATT. Attribution may then use Apple-permitted non-tracking measurement such as SKAdNetwork.

The Service Provider does not fingerprint the device (and does not combine IP, device signals, or other data) to identify you across apps or websites after you deny tracking. You can change the choice later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Other iOS identifiers

IDFV may be processed to distinguish app installs on your device for analytics, crashes, and attribution that does not require ATT. IDFV is not used for personalized ads.

Sign in with Apple and account deletion (Guidelines 4.8 and 5.1.1(v))

If this iOS build offers sign-in, Sign in with Apple is offered as an option. If a third-party login is also offered on iOS, Sign in with Apple is available as an equivalent option.

Apple may provide an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, a name and email. Hide My Email uses Apple’s private relay. This information is used only to create and maintain the session, restore purchases or progress where applicable, and answer support you start — not for marketing. Apple’s policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If this iOS build lets you create or link an account, you can delete that account in the Application where an account-deletion control is provided. You may also request deletion at scollins@gmail.com. Deletion removes the account identifiers the Service Provider maintains. Apple may retain Sign in with Apple records under Apple’s policy. You can also revoke the app in Apple ID → Sign in with Apple.

Do not read the Google Play “Google Sign-In” paragraph as applying to the iOS binary unless Google Sign-In is actually included in that iOS binary.

In-app purchases (StoreKit)

If this iOS build includes in-app purchases, Apple processes payment via StoreKit / the App Store. Prices are shown by the App Store before you pay. The Service Provider receives purchase status, product identifiers, and receipts or tokens so the purchased features can be unlocked or restored — not to take a second payment outside Apple. Card numbers stay with Apple. The Application does not use fake restore buttons, disguised ads as buy buttons, or other manipulative purchase flows. Manage billing, refunds, and subscriptions in your Apple ID / App Store account. Restore previous purchases with the restore control in the Application or via Apple. Paid functionality does not require you to allow tracking or to share additional personal data.

Notifications on iOS

If this iOS build includes push, iOS will ask permission before notifications are sent (FCM / APNs). You can deny or later disable them in Settings → Notifications → the Application. The Application still works if notifications are off. Notifications are product messages (for example reminders), not marketing to a collected email list.

App Privacy labels (iOS)

In App Store Connect, data types should match this policy. Typical types, if present in the binary: Identifiers (Device ID / User ID), Usage Data, Diagnostics, Purchases, and Contact Info only if sign-in shares email/name. Location is not collected.
